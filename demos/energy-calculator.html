Formula used

kwh  = watts × hours × days ÷ 1000
cost = kwh × tariff            (2 dp)
carbon = kwh × carbon_factor   (3 dp)

All values are rounded half-up like Python's Decimal, avoiding binary floating-point surprises.

Validation rules carried over from Python

  • Negative inputs are rejected.Checked
  • Hours per day cannot exceed 24.Checked
  • Estimates are transparent and auditable.Design
