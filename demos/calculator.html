How the demo works

  • Grammar check — exactly three tokens with a supported operator, or an error is shown.Safe
  • Finite operands — non-finite numbers are rejected before arithmetic.Checked
  • Zero division — reported as cannot divide by zero.Handled
  • Precision — results are rounded to a predictable fixed point, like Decimal in Python.Simulation
