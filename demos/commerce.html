Reserve, pay, and compensate

An interactive demo of the commerce workflow: inventory reservation, payment, and compensating release on failure. Fictional goods, fictional money, real patterns.

Architecture (in-process model)

Client / Orchestrator

Sends an order for a SKU and a quantity.

↓ placeOrder(sku, qty)

Inventory service

Reserves stock (reserve) or rejects when unavailable.

if reserved → payment(total)

Payment service

Accepts, declines, or raises an uncertain exception.

accepted → CONFIRMED · declined/exception → stock RELEASED

Outcome

Order confirmed with a total, or rejected with a reason. Stock is never lost.

Honest boundary. This is a process-local reference workflow. There is no durable saga, payment gateway, or deployed service behind it.
